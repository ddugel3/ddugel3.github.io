I am an undergraduate student in the School of Software at Kookmin University. I was an undergraduate research assistant at the Machine Intelligence Laboratory, advised by Prof. Jaekoo Lee.

My research interests lie in embodied AI, robot locomotion, and efficient machine learning. I am currently interested in terrain-aware cross-embodiment locomotion: shared low-level control policies that generalize across diverse robot morphologies and unseen terrains.
